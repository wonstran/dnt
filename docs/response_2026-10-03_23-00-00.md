# Spec revision 4: findings from prototyping the dedup plan

Spec: [2026-10-03-track-dedup-design.md](superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-track-dedup-design.md) (rev. 4, new §12)  
Follows: [review_2026-10-03_21-45-14.md](review_2026-10-03_21-45-14.md) (no further design comments on rev. 3)

This is not a response to a review. Before writing the implementation plan I ran a throwaway prototype of the signals and the apply procedure on the three raw pedestrian files (`/mnt/e/videos/miami/dets`, fps 10) and looked at the video frames. It found a defect that rev. 3 could not show on paper, so I amended the spec instead of planning around it. The prototype lives in the session scratchpad and is not part of the repository.

## What the prototype showed

- **Measured counts.** 122/134 has `n = 13 / 89` and 125/134 has `n = 30 / 13`; so 125/134 is `6/13 = 0.46`. The rev. 3 text treated `0.20` as a lower bound and the pair's fate as open; both are replaced by the measured value.
- **Rev. 3 rule 1 fails the motivating case.** 125/134 has co-motion 0.29, scores about 0.02, and would be `AUTO_REJECT`. Rule 1 made any rejection a cannot-link pair, so it blocked 122/125/134 from forming one track, even though 122/125 and 122/134 both look right. At the starting thresholds 122/134 also scored 0.79, below `accept_above` 0.80, so 134 stayed out regardless.
- **Ground truth.** I rendered frames 700, 740, 780 and 820 with the three tracks drawn and asked the project owner, who confirmed that all three tracks are one person.

## Changes

## Prototype results (not the implementation)

At `accept_above` 0.75, `cooccur_hi` 0.50 and the amended rule: raw 122, 125 and 134 merge into 122 with 13 rows dropped; middle 266/270 (co-occupancy 0.53) and 15/19 (0.62) stay gated; 257/259 is a candidate that scores about 0.08 and is auto-rejected. Per clip, tracks before and after, merges applied, rows dropped: beginning 63 to 62 (1, 1 row), middle 89 to 83 (6, 10 rows), evening 134 to 129 (5, 19 rows). At `accept_above` 0.80 the evening result is `{122, 125}` only, because 122/134 scores 0.79.

## Not verified

- The margin on `cooccur_hi` is thin: 0.46 (the pair that must pass) against 0.53 (the nearest pair that must stay gated), from three clips. A different clip could break it, and the plan's calibration task does not make it robust, only re-checks it.
- I did not check whether the other merges the prototype applied (for example middle 71/78/79, evening 149/150, 443/446, 535/553) are correct. I looked at one group on video.
- Nothing in the repository was implemented or run; the existing 1149 refine tests pass on the unchanged tree.
